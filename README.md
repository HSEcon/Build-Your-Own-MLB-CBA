# Build Your Own MLB CBA

Independent sports economics research and interactive CBA simulator.

Player analytics are calculated from Retrosheet game records through 2025; 2027 is an illustrative two-year scenario, not observed performance or a verified roster. No proprietary DRS, OAA, fWAR, or bWAR data are redistributed. The prior Phase 1 public financial inputs remain the baseline. Deployment is gated on actual-data browser validation.

The information used here was obtained free of charge from and is copyrighted by Retrosheet. Interested parties may contact Retrosheet at "www.retrosheet.org". See https://www.retrosheet.org/notice.txt.
